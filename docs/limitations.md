# Interpretation limits

The four named reference assemblies were selected for a reproducible technical demonstration. They are not a population sample. Assembly QC cannot establish completeness, contamination, species identity, structural accuracy or read-level quality. RefSeq assemblies may include plasmids; gene detection may vary with assembly and database versions. A detected gene does not establish expression, toxin production, clinical resistance or infection severity. A missing call may reflect database coverage, divergence, fragmentation or pipeline threshold.

No authentic AST phenotype, collection year or patient acquisition setting was verified consistently across the cohort. Consequently, no community-versus-healthcare comparison, genotype–phenotype concordance, SCCmec classification, sequence type distribution, transmission inference or prevalence estimate is reported. AMRFinderPlus, its reference database, MLST software/database and an SCCmec typing workflow were unavailable on the validation host; these analyses are **not performed**, not negative. The absence of result files must never be interpreted as absence of a determinant.
