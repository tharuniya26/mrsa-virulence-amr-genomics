# Interpretation limits

The four named reference assemblies were selected for a reproducible technical demonstration. They are not a population sample. Assembly QC cannot establish completeness, contamination or read-level quality. RefSeq assemblies may include plasmids; gene detection may vary with assembly and database versions. A detected gene does not establish expression, toxin production, clinical resistance or infection severity. A missing call may reflect database coverage, divergence, fragmentation or pipeline threshold. No authentic AST phenotype, collection year or patient acquisition setting was verified for the cohort. Consequently, no community versus healthcare comparison, genotype–phenotype concordance, SCCmec classification, sequence type distribution or transmission inference is reported. AMRFinderPlus and virulence screening are pending on a compatible bioinformatics environment.
