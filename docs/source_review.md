# Review of the historical source

The original DOCX is retained unchanged in the private parent workspace. Its MRSA material is in paragraphs 304–324; the remainder includes a separate breast cancer case, assignment pages and unrelated references. The public repository does not contain the DOCX or embedded source figures.

| Source location | Relevant claim | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| §4.1, paragraph 306 | *S. aureus* is opportunistic; mecA on SCCmec encodes low β-lactam affinity PBP2a | Broad mechanism is supported by [Foster 2004](https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI23825) and [NCBI's MRSA review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6148192/). “Mutant PBP” is imprecise: PBP2a is an acquired alternative PBP. |
| §4.1, paragraph 306 | SCCmec IV contains only mecA, regulators and ccrAB | Too narrow. SCCmec IV has variable joining regions and subtype structure; assignment requires cassette typing, not mecA alone. |
| §4.2, paragraph 312 | A particular infection was tetracycline susceptible because resistance genes were absent | No patient isolate accession, AST record or genome is supplied. This cannot be tested or carried into the new results. Absence of a detected gene does not prove susceptibility. |
| §4.3, paragraph 319 | CA-MRSA normally carries PVL and SCCmec IV | Overgeneralized. These are historically associated with some lineages, but neither defines acquisition setting. [PVL systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3530297/). |
| §4.3, paragraph 319 | HA-MRSA is more virulent; PVL explains clinical severity | Unsupported as a general rule. Virulence is strain, host and setting dependent; gene presence does not establish toxin production or causal severity. |
| Conclusion, paragraph 324 | The patient strain was “genetically simple” and tetracycline genes absent | No isolate-level genomic evidence. Excluded. |

The source's MRSA-relevant bibliography includes Alkharsah et al. 2018, Aman & Adhikari 2014, Asghar 2014, Bakthavatchalam et al. 2017, Chukwudi 2016, Foster 2004, Graber 2021, Grebe et al. 2024 and Shalaby et al. 2020. The original citation formatting has some typographic errors; DOI, scope and figure provenance require manual verification before reuse. New background relies on independently linked literature and NCBI documentation. Breast cancer citations were excluded.

Missing from the source: isolate IDs, assembly accessions, raw sequencing records, AST measurements, gene-call methods, QC, sampling design and reproducible computational analysis. This project adds a traceable public reference-assembly cohort, QC code, AMRFinderPlus integration and restrained comparative analysis. The historical clinical scenario is not presented as an observed dataset.
